Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026 (usage numbers, referrals, support messages and the monthly email added)

The short version

FilesBolt works on your own computer. The files you merge, compress, convert, sign or otherwise change with FilesBolt are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Your files

All processing happens locally on your PC. We don't receive, store or have access to your files or their contents. New files are saved where you choose, by default next to the originals.

Emails we send

Besides sign-in and password codes, we send two short emails near the end of your free trial: one 5 days before it ends and one the day before. Each has a link to turn these off. We don't send marketing email or share your address.

Error reports

If something goes wrong in the app, FilesBolt sends us a short error report so we can fix it. It contains the error message, where in FilesBolt's code it happened, the app and Windows version, the type of file involved (for example ".pdf") and an anonymous code for your PC. Before sending, FilesBolt removes file names, folder names and email addresses. File contents are never sent. Reports are deleted after 90 days. You can turn error reports off in FilesBolt's Settings.

This website

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to filesbolt.com, such as which pages are viewed and which countries visitors come from. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other sites and doesn't identify you. The download itself is served by GitHub, which keeps a count of downloads.

What FilesBolt stores on your computer

FilesBolt keeps a small settings file on your PC (in your user's AppData folder). It holds your choices, such as the save folder, light or dark mode, your watermark style, which actions appear in the right-click menu and how often you use each action, so the most-used ones appear first. This file never leaves your computer. Uninstalling FilesBolt removes the app and its right-click menu.

Your account

To use FilesBolt you create a free account with your email address. For your account we store:

  • your email address, and your password in scrambled (hashed) form that nobody, including us, can read
  • when your free trial started and ends, and your plan
  • how many jobs you've used this month. Only the number: never file names, contents or any details about your files
  • usage numbers for each month: how many jobs and files you ran with each tool (for example "Merge: 4 jobs, 9 files") and the estimated time saved. These power the Usage page in Settings and your monthly summary email. They never include file names, contents or folders
  • if you use Refer a friend: your invite code, and which account joined with it, so we can give the free month
  • if you contact support from the app: your message, the topic you picked, your app version and Windows version
  • for each PC you sign in on: a one-way scrambled code that identifies the PC, its computer name (for example DESKTOP-4F2K9), and when it was first and last used. This lets each PC get one free trial, keeps your account signed in on one PC at a time, and lets support see your PCs if you ask us to reset them

Account data is stored with our service provider Supabase. Sign-up codes and password reset codes are emailed from no-reply@filesbolt.com through Google Workspace. On your PC, FilesBolt keeps only a sign-in token in Windows Credential Manager, never your password.

We use this data only to run your account and plan. We don't sell it or share it for advertising. Besides sign-in codes, we email you trial reminders, a short monthly summary of your usage, and a note when you earn a free month from a referral. Every one of these emails has an unsubscribe link.

To delete your account and its data, open FilesBolt, click your name at the bottom left (this opens Settings, Account), and choose Delete account. You'll confirm with your password and a code we email you. You can also email support@filesbolt.com from the address you signed up with.

What we don't collect

Apart from the job and usage numbers above, the FilesBolt app doesn't collect analytics, tracking data, or anything about your files.

This website doesn't use cookies or tracking. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and the download is served by GitHub. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit or download. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Future features

We plan to add paid plans. Payments will be handled by a payment provider, and we'll never see or store your card number. If we add any feature that sends more information over the internet, we'll explain clearly what it sends before you use it, and we'll update this policy first.

Children

FilesBolt isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
