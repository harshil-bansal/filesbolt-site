Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026 (accounts added)

The short version

FilesBolt works on your own computer. The files you merge, compress, convert, sign or otherwise change with FilesBolt are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Your files

All processing happens locally on your PC. We don't receive, store or have access to your files or their contents. New files are saved where you choose, by default next to the originals.

What FilesBolt stores on your computer

FilesBolt keeps a small settings file on your PC (in your user's AppData folder). It holds your choices, such as the save folder, light or dark mode, your watermark style, which actions appear in the right-click menu and how often you use each action, so the most-used ones appear first. This file never leaves your computer. Uninstalling FilesBolt removes the app and its right-click menu.

Your account

To use FilesBolt you create a free account with your email address. For your account we store:

  • your email address, and your password in scrambled (hashed) form that nobody, including us, can read
  • when your free trial started and ends, and your plan
  • how many jobs you've used this month. Only the number: never file names, contents or any details about your files
  • an anonymous identifier for your PC (a one-way scrambled code, not your PC's name), so each PC gets one free trial

Account data is stored with our service provider Supabase. Sign-up codes and password reset codes are emailed from no-reply@filesbolt.com through Google Workspace. On your PC, FilesBolt keeps only a sign-in token in Windows Credential Manager, never your password.

We use this data only to run your account and plan. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or send marketing email.

To delete your account and its data, email support@filesbolt.com from the address you signed up with.

What we don't collect

The FilesBolt app doesn't collect analytics, tracking data, or anything about your files.

This website doesn't use cookies or tracking. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and the download is served by GitHub. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit or download. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Future features

We plan to add paid plans. Payments will be handled by a payment provider, and we'll never see or store your card number. If we add any feature that sends more information over the internet, we'll explain clearly what it sends before you use it, and we'll update this policy first.

Children

FilesBolt isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@filesbolt.com.
