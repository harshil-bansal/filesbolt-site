About FilesBolt

FilesBolt is a Windows app for the everyday file jobs that usually send people to an online PDF website: merging, splitting, compressing, converting and signing PDFs, photos and Excel files. It works on your own PC, from the right-click menu or its own window, so your files are never uploaded anywhere.

Who makes it

FilesBolt is made by Harshil Bansal, an independent developer. He built it so that merging or shrinking a PDF never means uploading private documents to a stranger's website.

What we believe

  • Your files are yours. FilesBolt processes them on your computer and never sends them to us.
  • Simple beats clever. Right-click, pick an action, done.
  • We keep improving it. See what's new, and tell us what you need.
