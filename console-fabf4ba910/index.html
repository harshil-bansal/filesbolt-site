Payments

    
Payments for FilesBolt Pro: cards worldwide through Stripe, and UPI or cards in India through Razorpay.
      Receipts and invoices are Stripe's own documents (Razorpay payments have none here; see the Razorpay dashboard).
      UPI plans can be cancelled but not paused. A full refund ends Pro straight away and stops renewal; a partial refund leaves the plan as it is.
      Every refund and change is written to Activity.

      If a customer has replaced their PCs, reset their devices here so they can sign in on new ones.
      Each PC can be used with at most 2 accounts: use Free up to let one more in, or Shared PC for offices and families (up to 10).

Restore a deleted account's email

    
When someone deletes their account, that email can't sign up again. Restore it here after they contact support:
      they get an email saying they can sign up again. Their new account starts on the Free plan, with no free trial or
      launch freebies; give them more from Accounts > Manage or with a coupon.

Send an email

    
Goes to everyone who gets Tips, news and offers (… people).
      Write plain text; a blank line starts a new paragraph. You can insert each person's
      {first_name}, {invite_code} and
      {invite_link}. Every email ends with a link to change choices or unsubscribe.
      Emails go out about 80 an hour; you can stop a send until it finishes.
