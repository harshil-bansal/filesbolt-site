Make photos smaller for email, forms and uploads

Right-click a photo and choose FilesBolt → Resize for email (under 500 KB). In the app you can also pick Small, Medium or Large, or a file size limit such as under 100 KB, which many online forms ask for.

How to do it

  1. Right-click the photos.
  2. Choose FilesBolt → Resize for email (under 500 KB).
  3. A smaller copy is saved next to each photo; the original isn't changed.

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Can I resize to under 100 KB for a government form?

Yes. In the app choose Resize images → Under 100 KB.

Will my phone photos be sideways?

No. They're turned the right way up before resizing.
