FilesBolt Pro

Unlimited use of every FilesBolt tool, on all your devices (Windows, Mac and Android). Everything still happens on your own device; your files are never uploaded.

US$2.99 a month, by debit or credit card. Local taxes may be added where required. UPI for India is coming soon.

Cancel any time: you keep Pro until the end of the month you've paid for. New to paying? Our 14-day money-back guarantee covers your first payment.

Free plan

Every new account starts with a free trial with every tool. After it, the free plan includes 10 jobs a month. One job is one action, such as one merge or one compression, however many files it includes.

How to upgrade

Open FilesBolt, go to Settings > Account and choose Upgrade to Pro. The secure payment page opens in your browser, and FilesBolt switches to Pro within a minute after you pay.

Who you pay

Payments are handled by Stripe, which sells FilesBolt Pro for us as the merchant of record and takes care of sales tax and VAT. FilesBolt itself is provided by Lumisha LLC. We never see or store your card details. Some Indian cards don't allow automatic monthly renewals from companies abroad; if yours doesn't, you can pay again each month, or wait for UPI.
