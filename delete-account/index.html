Delete your FilesBolt account

FilesBolt is made by Lumisha. You can delete your FilesBolt account and its data at any time, from any device you use FilesBolt on.

In the app

  • Android: open FilesBolt, tap Account, then Delete my account.
  • iPhone and iPad: open FilesBolt, tap the account icon, then Delete my account.
  • Windows and Mac: open FilesBolt, go to Settings and choose Delete account.

You'll confirm with your password and a code we email you. The account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email support@filesbolt.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm by email once it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, password and two-step sign-in settings.
  • Your plan, coupon time, invite code and usage counts.
  • The list of devices signed in to your account.

Your files are never on our servers: FilesBolt works on your device, so there are no files to delete.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A short record of the deletion (email, name, sign-up date and deletion date) for 90 days, then it's removed.
  • Support messages and tickets: 2 years after the conversation is closed.
  • If you paid for Pro: payment records (amount, date and invoice) for as long as tax law requires.

More details are in our privacy policy. Questions? Contact us.
