Convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG

Select the HEIC photos, right-click and choose FilesBolt → Convert to JPG. JPG copies appear next to them, the right way up.

How to do it

  1. Select the .heic photos in File Explorer.
  2. Right-click: FilesBolt → Convert to JPG.
  3. The JPGs are saved next to the originals.

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Do I need to install a HEIC extension from Microsoft?

No. FilesBolt reads HEIC by itself.

Can I make a PDF from HEIC photos?

Yes: choose Convert to PDF or Merge into one PDF.
