Sign and fill PDFs on your PC

Right-click a PDF and choose FilesBolt → Edit and sign. Draw or type your signature once, place it, add text or today's date, and save. A signed copy is saved next to the original.

How to do it

  1. Right-click the PDF and choose FilesBolt → Edit and sign.
  2. Add your signature, text, date or check marks.
  3. Click Save: a _signed copy appears next to the original.

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Is my signature uploaded?

No. It's saved only on your PC so you can reuse it.

Can I white out or move things on the page?

Yes: whiteout, and Select lets you move, copy, delete or crop part of a page.
