Add a password to a PDF, on your PC

Right-click a PDF and choose FilesBolt → Protect with a password. The protected copy needs the password to open.

How to do it

  1. Right-click the PDF.
  2. Choose FilesBolt → Protect with a password.
  3. Type the password; a protected copy is saved.

Download FilesBolt for Windows

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Can I remove a password?

Yes, if you know it: use Remove password in the app.

Is the password sent anywhere?

No. It never leaves your PC.
