Merge PDFs into one file, without uploading them

Select the files in File Explorer, right-click and choose FilesBolt → Merge into one PDF. The merged PDF appears next to your files in a second or two. Nothing is uploaded.

How to do it

  1. Select the PDFs, photos or Word files in File Explorer.
  2. Right-click and choose FilesBolt → Merge into one PDF.
  3. Find Merged.pdf in the same folder.

Download FilesBolt for Windows

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Can I change the order?

Yes. Open FilesBolt, add the files, drag them into the order you want and click Merge.

Can I merge photos and Word files too?

Yes. Photos (JPG, PNG, HEIC and more) and Office files are turned into PDF pages as they're merged.

Is there a file size or page limit?

No. Everything runs on your PC, so there's no upload limit.
