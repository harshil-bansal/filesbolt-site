Turn photos into a PDF, without uploading them

Select your photos, right-click and choose FilesBolt → Convert to PDF for one PDF per photo, or Merge into one PDF to put them all in one file. Phone photos are turned the right way up.

How to do it

  1. Select the photos in File Explorer.
  2. Right-click: FilesBolt → Convert to PDF (or Merge into one PDF).
  3. The PDF is saved next to the photos.

Download FilesBolt for Windows

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Does it work with iPhone HEIC photos?

Yes, HEIC and HEIF are supported, along with JPG, PNG, WEBP, TIFF and more.

Is there a limit on how many photos?

No. Everything runs on your PC.
