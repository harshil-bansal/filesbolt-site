Refund policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Try it free first

Every new account gets 30 days of FilesBolt with every tool and no limits, so you can make sure it works for you before you pay anything.

14-day money-back guarantee

If FilesBolt isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment for a plan and we'll refund it in full. No questions asked.

  • Monthly and yearly plans: the guarantee covers your first payment. Renewals aren't refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Lifetime plans: refundable within 14 days of purchase.
  • If something is wrong: if you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or FilesBolt doesn't work on your PC and we can't fix it, we'll refund you at any time.

How to ask

Email support@filesbolt.com from the address on your account and tell us which payment you'd like refunded. We'll reply within 2 business days. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually show up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

When a payment is refunded, the paid time it bought ends, and the account moves to the free plan.

Coupon codes

Free time from coupon codes isn't a payment, so there's nothing to refund.
