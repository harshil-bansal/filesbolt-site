Combine Excel and CSV files into one workbook

Select the files, right-click and choose FilesBolt → Combine into one Excel file. In the app you can choose one sheet per file, or all rows in one sheet.

How to do it

  1. Select the .xlsx or .csv files.
  2. Right-click: FilesBolt → Combine into one Excel file.
  3. Open Combined.xlsx in the same folder.

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Does it keep formatting?

Values, formulas and basic formatting are kept.

Can I split a workbook into files?

Yes: Split sheets saves each sheet as its own file.
