Split a PDF into pages, on your PC

Right-click a PDF and choose FilesBolt → Split into pages to save every page as its own PDF, or open FilesBolt to pick just the pages you want.

How to do it

  1. Right-click the PDF.
  2. Choose FilesBolt → Split into pages.
  3. The pages are saved in a new folder next to it.

Download FilesBolt for Windows

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Can I pick only some pages?

Yes. In the app choose Split pages and tick the pages you need.

Can I reorder or remove pages instead?

Yes, use Organize pages.
