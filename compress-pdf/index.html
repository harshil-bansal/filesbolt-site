Make PDFs smaller, without uploading them

Right-click a PDF and choose FilesBolt → Compress. You get a smaller copy next to the original, and FilesBolt tells you when a file is already as small as it can be.

How to do it

  1. Right-click the PDF (or several) in File Explorer.
  2. Choose FilesBolt → Compress.
  3. The smaller copy is saved next to the original.

Download FilesBolt for Windows

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Will the quality drop?

Text stays sharp. Pictures inside the PDF are resized to what's needed on the page, and you can pick Balanced or Smallest in the app.

Why didn't my file get smaller?

Some PDFs are already compact (mostly text). FilesBolt never gives you a bigger file.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Compression happens on your computer.
