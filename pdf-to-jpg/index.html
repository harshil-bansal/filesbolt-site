Turn PDF pages into JPG pictures on your PC

Right-click a PDF and choose FilesBolt → Convert to JPG. Each page becomes a sharp JPG in the same folder.

How to do it

  1. Right-click the PDF.
  2. Choose FilesBolt → Convert to JPG.
  3. One JPG per page appears next to the PDF.

Download FilesBolt for Windows

Why offline?

Online PDF websites make you upload your files to their servers. FilesBolt does the same jobs on your own computer, so your contracts, IDs and invoices never leave it. It works without internet, with no file size limits and no waiting for uploads.

Questions

Can I get PNG instead?

Yes. Open FilesBolt and choose PNG.

What about password-protected PDFs?

FilesBolt asks for the password first.
